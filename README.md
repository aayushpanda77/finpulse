# FinPulse — Smart Loan & Credit Checker

An interactive, beginner-friendly **FinTech Loan & Credit Checker** web app built by **Ayush Panda**.

Move the sliders for **Yearly Income**, **Loan Needed**, **Monthly Bills %**, **Credit Card Used %**, **Years Using Credit**, and **Missed Payments** to instantly see:

- **Safe Loan Limit** & extra safe borrowing room
- **Credit Score (out of 850)** & chance of missing a payment
- **Monthly Payment (EMI)** & interest rate over 3 years
- **Loan Approval & Safety Trend Chart** over 36 months
- **Plain-English Breakdown Table** showing which habits help or hurt loan approval

---

## Features

1. **Live Interactive Sliders**: Adjust 6 everyday financial numbers and watch every card, chart, and table update in real time.
2. **1-Click Sample People**: Test preset profiles (`Safe User`, `New Earner`, `Average User`, `Risky User`) or generate a `+ Random` person.
3. **Interest Rate Stress Test**: Toggle `+2% Rate` to see how higher interest rates change monthly EMI and loan safety.
4. **Dark / Light Theme Toggle**: Switch between the clean white FinTech dashboard theme and dark mode.
5. **Save Report (`.json`)**: Export your full loan evaluation summary with one click.
